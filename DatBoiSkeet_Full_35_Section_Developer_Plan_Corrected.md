**Goal:** Turn DatBoiSkeet into a music, social media, livestreaming, advertising, and creator-earning platform. Fans can build followers, post content, go live, promote products, and earn money while helping grow DBS.

Preserve the existing design, branding, memberships, and functionality.

## 1. DBS Posts — Social Network

Rename Reels to Posts.

## 2. DBS Clippers — Application & Earning System

All Clippers must register, apply, and receive admin approval.

Application collects:
- Name, email, and age.
- TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, and X usernames/profile links.
- Followers/subscribers on each platform.
- Monthly views and average views per post.
- Monthly likes, comments, shares, saves, and engagement rates.
- Posting frequency, content niche, promotional experience, and work samples.
- Analytics screenshots or supported social account connections.

**No minimum follower counts or automatic approvals.**

Approval process:
1. Submit application and analytics.
2. Admin approves, rejects, or waitlists.
3. Approved Clippers access paid campaigns.
4. Download authorized DBS footage.
5. Create clips and promote DBS with tracked links.
6. Submit posts and performance reports.
7. Admin verifies results and approves payments.

**Clipper Dashboard:** Campaigns, deadlines, downloads, referral links, verified views, signups, sales, rankings, and earnings.

Clippers receive approved campaign payments or referral commissions. DBS may charge a disclosed campaign-management fee on third-party-funded promotions.

## 3. DBS Live — Creator Livestreaming

## 4. DBS Marketplace — Advertising

### Live Gift Battles — Viewer vs. Viewer

- While watching a livestream, a fan who sends a gift or voluntary tip may challenge another participating fan to a **60-second gift battle**. The challenger selects the recipient, who must accept. The receiving fan must be able to decline, disable invitations, or block challenges.
- Display a large **1:00 countdown**, both fans' display names/avatar icons, each fan's verified eligible gift/tip total for the battle, and a clearly labeled live score.
