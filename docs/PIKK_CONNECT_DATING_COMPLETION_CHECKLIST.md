# PIKK Connect & Dating — Production Completion Checklist

Status: NOT COMPLETE. Existing UI/prototypes do not constitute working features.

## Connect (must work end to end)
- [ ] Persistent user profiles, follow/unfollow, follower counts and privacy controls
- [ ] Create/edit/delete posts with photos, video, text, hashtags, media authorization
- [ ] Reels/shorts and 24-hour Stories with reliable upload/storage/expiry
- [ ] Likes, comments, replies, reposts, shares, saves and abuse protection
- [ ] Real-time 1:1 and group messages, delivery/read status and notifications
- [ ] Community rooms: Ladies Lounge, Girl Talk, Business, Money Talk, Conspiracy, music/A&R, and user-created eligible rooms
- [ ] Discover/search accounts, posts and communities
- [ ] Random video chat (Monkey-style): opt-in, matching, skip, report, block, age protections, moderation
- [ ] Creator networking, fan alerts, user notifications and settings
- [ ] Moderation queues, privacy, block/report, spam controls, accessibility
- [ ] Backend data persistence, cross-device tests, mobile tests, performance and security

## Dating (must work end to end)
- [ ] Separate opt-in dating profile and age-eligibility verification
- [ ] Dating interests, preferences, categories, poly options and safe filters
- [ ] Swipe discovery, like/pass and mutual matches
- [ ] Match-based private messages and match notifications
- [ ] Unmatch, block, report and moderation/escalation
- [ ] Location privacy (never reveal precise location by default), visibility controls and consent
- [ ] Privacy disclosures, safety information and affirmative dating terms
- [ ] Secure backend storage, account deletion and sensitive-data handling
- [ ] QA across devices, accessibility, abuse scenarios and reliability

## Cross-platform rule
- PIKK Free ($0 forever) may open all seven sections, including Connect and Dating. Dating participation requires age eligibility and opt-in. Creator/artist/streamer/seller roles do not remove access to other sections.
- Do not call either section complete until real accounts can use the features end-to-end, with backend persistence, safety and tests.
- Show these as major outstanding workstreams on the master backlog and progress meter. Do not claim live deployment until verified.
