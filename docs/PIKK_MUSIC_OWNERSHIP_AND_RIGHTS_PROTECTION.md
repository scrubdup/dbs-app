# PIKK Music — Permanent Downloads & Rights Protection (planned)
Status: PRODUCT SPECIFICATION ONLY. The live demo has no paid checkout, licensed media delivery, automated fingerprinting, Content ID access, registration agency or royalty-distribution system.

## Customer purchase model
- Brand: **PIKK Music — Buy It. Download It. Keep It.**
- Artist decides whether to offer streaming, ownership downloads, or both; sets pricing.
- Suggested USD prices, not mandatory: single $1.29; exclusive single $1.99; EP $5.99; album $9.99; deluxe $14.99; collector album $19.99.
- Proposed direct-sales split: **85% artist, 15% PIKK** before processor fees, taxes, refunds, chargebacks and any other applicable deductions. Review margins and payment marketplace rules before finalization.
- DRM-free MP3 and optionally FLAC with a perpetual personal-use license. Buyer does not acquire copyright. Files previously downloaded remain usable after canceling subscription. Re-download depends on account/service availability, licensing terms and retention.
- A demo “Add to My Music” is NOT payment, proof of rights or a downloadable file. Explicit prelaunch disclosure required.
- Checkout must verify entitlements server-side. Signed, expiring download URLs, paid receipts, refund logic and auditable rights provenance are required before real sales.
- Prevent publishing where rights holder has not confirmed rights to sell permanent downloads (master and composition clearances, samples, beats, producer agreements).

## PIKK Rights & Protection
**Tagline:** Your Music. Your Rights. Your Revenue.
The artist retains their copyright. PIKK does not grant copyrights, trademarks, BMI membership or government registration and must not present itself as a performing-rights organization.

### Phase 1 — rights registry
- Per release: artist identity, master owner, composition rights owners and songwriter/publisher splits, producer and beat licenses, sample clearance, authorized distributors, ISRC if assigned, uploaded master and permissions.
- Store signed attestations and supporting documents securely with granular access controls, timestamps, audit log and a correction/dispute workflow; do not publicly display private legal paperwork.
- Copyright registration assistant can link to official Copyright Office channels and explain necessary documents; filing must be authorized and is not automatic.

### Phase 2 — upload protections
- Generate robust audio fingerprints from authorized master references.
- Test exact copies, pitch shift, tempo shift, chopped/short excerpts, overlays, remasters and transcoding; set empirically validated confidence thresholds.
- Matches route to *review*, not unconditional auto-bans, because co-owners, authorized covers/remixes, shared licensed beats and public-domain recordings create legitimate overlaps.
- Notify relevant rights holders, allow counter-evidence and human escalation/appeals, preserve evidentiary records, and prohibit abusive claims.
- A fingerprint does not prove underlying ownership or copyright validity.

### Phase 3 — external enforcement
- YouTube: apply for eligibility or work with an authorized Content ID partner; no blanket access guarantee. Only exclusive and appropriately controlled rights are eligible as reference material.
- Spotify, Apple Music and other services: collect grounded identifiers, evidence and alleged matches and help rights holders use platform-specific reports or distributor channels; no cross-platform blocking powers.
- For PIKK's own hosted content, define DMCA notice/counter-notice procedure, designated agent when applicable, repeat-infringer policy, user appeals and qualified legal review.
- Optional traceable buyer watermarking should respect privacy and should not interfere with lawful personal listening. DRM-free files can still be copied; deterrence and enforcement, not guaranteed piracy prevention.

### Platform UX
- Artist console: **Rights Registry**, **Audio Shield**, **Matches & Disputes**, **External Claims**, **Royalty Guide**.
- Clear states: Planned → Submitted → Verification pending → Verified reference → Match under review → Resolved; no unverified claim of protection.
- Artist-controlled permissions and split changes should be versioned; no automatic assignment of artist copyright to PIKK.
- Keep My Music and downloadable purchases accessible separately from paid memberships.
